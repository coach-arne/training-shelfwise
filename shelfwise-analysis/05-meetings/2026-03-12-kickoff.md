## 1. Goals and timeline

Hannah presented the project charter (BUS-01 v1.1). Go-live is planned for January 2027, all branches at once. LibTrack 4 vendor support ends on 31 March 2027, which leaves three months of margin.

## 2. Scope

- Scope as in the charter. Inter-library loans were discussed; they are explicitly phase 2.
- Ellen noted that youth members who borrow for school projects regularly reach their loan limit, and asked whether the limit could be raised. Hannah will consider this; **no decision**.
- Raj noted that the fine tariffs have not changed since 2019. A dedicated fines workshop will be organised in early April.

## 3. Architecture direction

- Sara presented the modular monolith (ADR-001, accepted) and the choice for MySQL (ADR-002).
- Pieter mentioned that city IT is moving to PostgreSQL for new applications. Sara will verify.

## 4. Privacy

Pieter stated that a DPIA is required and that the retention of loan history must be defined explicitly. Reading history is sensitive personal data in the eyes of most members.

## 5. Ways of working

- Analysis documents in the shared repository; changes are recorded in the CHANGELOG.
- Conflicts between documents are raised with the business analyst; conflicts that cannot be resolved go to the product owner.
