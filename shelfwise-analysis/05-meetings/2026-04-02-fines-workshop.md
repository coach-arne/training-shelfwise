## Context

The current fine rules of LibTrack 4 were reviewed, using the tariff table exported from LibTrack 4 (`06-reference/fine-tariffs.csv`). The goal was to agree on the fine policy for Shelfwise.

## Decisions

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | The ADULT daily rate for BOOK is raised from €0.20 to €0.25. AUDIOVISUAL (€1.00) and MAGAZINE (€0.10) are unchanged. YOUTH rates are unchanged (€0.10 / €0.50 / €0.05). |
| D2 | The grace period is reduced from 2 days to **1 opening day**. If a copy is returned after the grace period, the grace day is charged as well. |
| D3 | Only opening days count as chargeable days (unchanged). |
| D4 | New: SENIOR members receive a **50% discount** on overdue fines. |
| D5 | Rounding: half-up to the cent, applied once per copy, after the discount and before the maximum. |
| D6 | Maximum fine per copy: **€10.00 or the replacement cost of the copy, whichever is lower** (previously a flat €10.00). |
| D7 | Lost declaration unchanged: six weeks, that is **42 calendar days**, after the due date. Charges: replacement cost plus a €5.00 administration fee. |
| D8 | Blocking threshold confirmed: a member with an outstanding balance of **€5.00 or more** cannot borrow, renew or reserve. |
| D9 | Waivers: librarians up to €2.00 per charge, Branch Managers without limit. A reason is always mandatory. |

## Discussion notes

- Ellen asked whether youth members could become fine-free, as several libraries in the region have done. Raj was concerned about the revenue impact. Hannah will put this on the agenda of the steering committee.
- Jonas asked how rounding should work for the senior discount (for example €0.125). Answer: see D5, so €0.13.
