## 1. Project status

Analysis is nearly complete. The build phase starts in June as planned. Go-live in January 2027 is confirmed.

## 2. Youth fine policy

Following the fines workshop, the committee discussed a fine-free policy for youth members. Arguments in favour: the city's literacy policy, the regional trend, and evidence that fines discourage children from returning to the library. Raj estimated the revenue impact as limited, because most youth fines are on books.

## 3. ADR-005

Sara presented the problems with the nightly accrual job and the proposal to calculate fines on demand. The committee approved ADR-005.

## 4. Sizing

Sara noted that the sizing figures in the technical analysis need to be checked against the charter. The committee confirmed that the infrastructure must be planned for 85,000 active members in six branches, with 10% growth headroom.

## 5. Scope questions

A request from two branch managers to include inter-library loans in phase 1 was discussed. Given the timeline, inter-library loans remain phase 2.

## Decisions

| ID | Decision |

| SC-2 | ADR-005 (on-demand fine calculation) is approved. |
| SC-3 | Sizing: 85,000 active members, six branches, 10% growth headroom. |
| SC-4 | Inter-library loans remain out of scope for phase 1. |
| SC-5 | Go-live in January 2027 is confirmed. |
