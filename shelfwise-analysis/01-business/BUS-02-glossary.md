Terms are listed alphabetically. Use these terms consistently in all documents, user interfaces and communication.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Account balance | The sum of all unpaid charges on a member's account. Also "outstanding balance". |
| Active member | A member with a valid membership who borrowed at least one item in the last 24 months. |
| Blocked | Status of a member who may not borrow, renew or place reservations, for example because of an outstanding balance or an expired membership. A blocked member can still return items and pay. |
| Branch | One of the six physical library locations of NPL. |
| Charge | Any amount a member owes the library: an overdue fine, a replacement cost, an administration fee or a reservation fee. |
| Chargeable day | An opening day on which a loan is overdue. Only chargeable days count for overdue fines. |
| Closing day | A day on which the library is closed: every Sunday and every public holiday. |
| Copy | One physical item of a title, identified by a unique barcode. A title can have many copies. Informally also called "item". |
| Due date | The date on which a loan must be returned at the latest. |
| Grace period | A period of two days after the due date during which a returned item does not incur a fine. |
| Guardian | Parent or legal guardian of a youth member; receives notifications on the youth member's behalf. |
| Hold | A copy that is set aside at a branch for a member who reserved the title. |
| Home branch | The branch that owns a copy. |
| LibTrack 4 | The legacy lending system that Shelfwise replaces. |
| Loan | The lending of one copy to one member, from checkout until return. |
| Lost item | A copy that is declared lost because it has not been returned 42 calendar days after its due date. |
| Material type | Classification of a title that determines loan period and fine rate: BOOK, AUDIOVISUAL or MAGAZINE. |
| Member | A person registered with the library. Note: "patron" was used in LibTrack 4; do not use it in Shelfwise. |
| Member category | ADULT, YOUTH or SENIOR, determined by age. |
| Notify gateway | The city's shared messaging service for e-mail and SMS. |
| Opening day | Any day that is not a closing day. |
| Overdue | A loan that has not been returned after its due date. |
| Overdue fine | A charge for returning a copy after its due date. |
| PayBridge | Online payment service provider used for payments in the member portal. |
| Renewal | Extending the due date of an existing loan. |
| Replacement cost | The amount charged when a copy is lost; recorded per copy. |
| Reservation | A request by a member to borrow a title as soon as a copy becomes available. |
| Title | The bibliographic description of a work (title, author, ISBN, material type). |
| Waiver | Cancelling all or part of a charge by authorised staff. |
